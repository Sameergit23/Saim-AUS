# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the Saim-AUS community to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainers privately via GitHub (open a private security advisory or contact the
maintainer through the repository). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and
will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or reject
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
